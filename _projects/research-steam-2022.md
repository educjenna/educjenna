**Role:** Principal Investigator  
**Year:** 2022  
**Funder:** Korea Foundation for the Advancement of Science and Creativity (KOFAC)

### CODE RED

The 2022 program integrated Grade 6 curriculum and achievement standards through a localized problem-based STEAM experience. Activities incorporated virtual reality, augmented reality, picture books, and games, alongside a school-based STEAM club and teacher professional learning.

**Role & Contributions**
- Led proposal development and the teacher study group.
- Coordinated curriculum design, implementation, and data collection.
- Supported in-school STEAM activities and teacher workshops.
- Implemented the program as part of the two-year funded initiative that reached 549 students overall.
