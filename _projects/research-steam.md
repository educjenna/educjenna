**Period:** 2022–2023  
**Funder:** Korea Foundation for the Advancement of Science and Creativity (KOFAC)  
**Award:** $4,000

I wrote the proposal and led teachers in designing, implementing, and collecting data on STEAM programs for 549 students. The work connected localized curriculum design with emerging technologies and community-centered problems.
