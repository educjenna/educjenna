**Context:** Grade 6 · 2022

### Learning Design

A week-long digital learning camp introduced **249 Grade 6 students** to AI ethics, introductory machine learning, robotics, block-based coding, and digital literacy. Activities addressed both how AI technologies work and ethical issues such as reliability, bias, and potential misuse.

**Role & Contributions**
- Organized and facilitated the camp.
- Designed learning activities spanning AI, software, robotics, and digital citizenship.
- Supported students in connecting technical concepts with ethical questions about AI.
