**Year:** 2023  
**Funder:** Korea Foundation for the Advancement of Science and Creativity (KOFAC)

### AVATAR: The Way of Water and Land

The 2023 study group restructured the Grade 4 curriculum around a localized problem-based STEAM program. Learning experiences connected environmental themes with virtual reality, block-based coding, chatbot development, picture books, and the school garden.

**Role & Contributions**
- Led proposal development and the teacher study group.
- Coordinated curriculum design, implementation, and data collection.
- Supported teacher workshops and collaborative curriculum development.
- Implemented the program across participating classrooms as part of the two-year funded initiative that reached 549 students overall.
