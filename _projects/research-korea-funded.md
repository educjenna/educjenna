### STEAM Teachers’ Study Group (2022–2023)
**Funder:** Korea Foundation for the Advancement of Science and Creativity (KOFAC) · **Award:** $4,000

I wrote the proposal and led teachers in designing, implementing, and collecting data on STEAM programs for 549 students.

### Online Teaching and Learning Materials for ITDA (2022)
**Funder:** Korea Education and Research Information Service (KERIS) · **Award:** $6,000

I led teachers from six schools in developing nationwide online content based on student and teacher interviews and delivered teacher professional development.

### Innovative Teaching Methods Research (2021, 2022)
**Funder:** Seoul National University of Education · **Award:** $1,000 × 2

I developed blended and AI-enhanced learning materials informed by a survey of 445 students and 19 teachers.
