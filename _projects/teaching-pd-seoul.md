**Period:** 2023–2024  
**Role:** Leading Teacher of Educational Technology  
**Organization:** Seoul Seobu District Office of Education

### Context

District-wide professional learning supported educators in integrating generative AI and educational technology into classroom practice. This work included the 2023 invited session, **“Effective Classroom Integration of Generative AI.”**

**Role & Contributions**
- Facilitated workshops for educators across the district.
- Connected emerging generative-AI tools with practical instructional applications.
- Supported professional learning around educational technology integration.
