**Role:** Project Lead  
**Year:** 2022  
**Funder:** Korea Education and Research Information Service (KERIS)  
**Award:** $6,000

### Context

The ITDA project developed online and blended teaching-and-learning materials for nationwide use. Student and teacher interviews informed the design process, with attention to classroom needs and practical implementation.

**Role & Contributions**
- Led teachers from six schools in developing online teaching and learning content.
- Used student and teacher interviews to inform material development.
- Developed guidance for online/open educational resources and delivered teacher professional development.

### Professional Learning

The project was also presented in the 2022 invited talk **“Outcomes of Developing Online Teaching and Learning Resources for ITDA”** at KISTI in Jeju, South Korea.

### Outcome

The project was recognized as a **Top 10 Team Nationwide** in the KERIS ITDA Online Materials Project.
