**Role:** Principal Investigator  
**Period:** 2021, 2022  
**Funder:** Seoul National University of Education  
**Award:** $1,000 × 2

I developed blended and AI-enhanced learning materials informed by a survey of 445 students and 19 teachers.

The 2021 work addressed blended learning in overcrowded classrooms. The 2022 work explored technology-enhanced environmental education using tools such as coding, machine learning, and virtual learning environments.
