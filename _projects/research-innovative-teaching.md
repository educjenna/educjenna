**Role:** Principal Investigator  
**Period:** 2021, 2022  
**Funder:** Seoul National University of Education  
**Award:** $1,000 × 2

### 2021 — Blended Learning

The 2021 project examined blended-learning needs and practices across three schools. Survey data from **445 students and 19 teachers** informed the development of blended-learning materials designed for classroom use.

### 2022 — Technology-Enhanced Environmental Education

The 2022 project developed and implemented environmental-learning experiences with **58 elementary students**, integrating tools such as code.org, Teachable Machine, Easy EDU, and CoSpaces EDU.

**Role & Contributions**
- Led the funded research and instructional-development work.
- Collected and used student and teacher data to inform learning-material design.
- Developed blended and AI-enhanced learning materials across the two project years.
