**Role:** Graduate Research Assistant  
**Period:** Fall 2024–Spring 2026

### High School Computer Science
At Ben Davis University High School, I co-designed and studied a project-based learning unit in which students explored how AI could address a local school-bus route problem.

### Grade 2 Computational Thinking
At Bloomfield Elementary School, I developed instructed versus spontaneous embodied computational-thinking activities, collected and analyzed data, and co-authored the resulting manuscript.

### High School English Language Arts
At Jasper High School, I designed four generative-AI chatbots for argumentative writing, persuasive writing, and career preparation. I collected and analyzed student–AI interaction logs, reflections, AI-literacy surveys, and post-surveys and co-authored related scholarship.
