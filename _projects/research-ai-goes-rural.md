**Role:** Graduate Research Assistant  
**Period:** Fall 2024–Spring 2026

The project investigated and designed AI- and computing-supported learning experiences across multiple K–12 contexts.

### High School Computer Science

A project-based learning unit at Ben Davis University High School engaged students in exploring how AI could address a local school-bus route problem, connecting computer science learning with an authentic community issue.

**Role & Contributions**
- Co-designed the project-based learning unit with the classroom teacher.
- Contributed to implementation and study of the unit.

### Grade 2 Computational Thinking

The Bloomfield Elementary School study examined instructed and spontaneous approaches to embodied computational thinking among Grade 2 students.

**Role & Contributions**
- Developed embodied computational-thinking activities.
- Collected and analyzed classroom data.
- Co-authored the resulting manuscript.

### High School English Language Arts

At Jasper High School, generative AI was integrated across argumentative writing, persuasive writing, and career-preparation units. Four purpose-built chatbots supported work with claims, evidence, warrants, résumé writing, and mock interviews.

**Role & Contributions**
- Designed four generative-AI chatbots.
- Collected and analyzed student–AI interaction logs, written reflections, AI-literacy surveys, and post-surveys.
- Contributed to related publications and manuscripts.
