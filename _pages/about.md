Juhyun Park is a Ph.D. student in **Instructional Systems Technology at Indiana University Bloomington** and a former public elementary school teacher in South Korea. Her research examines how emerging technologies can be purposefully designed to support learners’ thinking, engagement, and agency across K–12 and teacher education.

Her current work spans **generative AI, embodied and tangible learning, computing education, and teacher education**. As a graduate researcher on NSF-funded initiatives including **SILICON** and **AI Goes Rural**, she collaborates with K–12 educators to design and study learning experiences in AI, computing, and STEM. She also teaches **EDUC-W200: Teaching with Technology** for pre-service teachers.

> **How can emerging technologies be designed to expand learners’ opportunities to think, engage, and act—rather than simply make learning more efficient?**

**Research interests:** Generative AI · Embodied Learning · Computing Education · Teacher Education
