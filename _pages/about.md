## Hi, I'm Juhyun Park.

I am a Ph.D. student in **Instructional Systems Technology at Indiana University Bloomington**. Before beginning my doctoral studies, I spent five years as a public elementary school teacher in South Korea. My research is grounded in those classroom experiences and in a question that emerged as educational technologies rapidly entered schools:

> **How can emerging technologies be designed to expand learners' opportunities to think, engage, and act—rather than simply make learning more efficient?**

This question has shaped my shift from a technology-first perspective toward a learning-first approach to instructional design. My current work explores how generative AI, robotics, tangible tools, and embodied activities can scaffold learners' thinking and make complex ideas more accessible and meaningful. Across these contexts, I am particularly interested in designing technology that **supports rather than replaces learners' cognitive and metacognitive work**.

As a graduate researcher on NSF-funded projects including **SILICON** and **AI Goes Rural**, I collaborate with K–12 educators to design and study learning experiences in AI, computing, and STEM. My work spans embodied approaches to computational thinking, community-based computing education, AI-supported writing and learning, and the design of AI scaffolds for lesson planning.

My research is closely connected to my teaching. As an Associate Instructor for **EDUC-W200: Teaching with Technology**, I work with pre-service teachers as they learn to make pedagogically grounded decisions about technology integration and design technology-enhanced learning experiences of their own.

### Research Interests

**Embodied Learning & Cognition**  
Designing tangible and embodied learning experiences that connect abstract computational concepts with physical interaction and experience.

**AI & Metacognitive Scaffolding**  
Investigating how generative AI can support reflection, judgment, self-regulation, and critical thinking without encouraging cognitive offloading.

**Community-Engaged K–12 STEM & Computing Education**  
Designing authentic, project-based and community-connected learning environments in which young learners engage with AI, computing, and STEM.

**Teacher Education & Technology Integration**  
Supporting pre-service and in-service teachers in designing pedagogically meaningful uses of emerging technologies.
