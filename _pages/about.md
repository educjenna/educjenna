Juhyun Park is a third-year Ph.D. student in Instructional Systems Technology at Indiana University Bloomington and a former public elementary school teacher in Seoul, South Korea. Her research examines how learners think and learn with emerging technologies, with two primary areas of focus: **embodied and tangible learning** and **GenAI-supported learning**. As a graduate researcher, she contributes to NSF-funded and community-engaged projects including [**SILICON**](/educjenna/projects/research-silicon/), [**AI Goes Rural**](/educjenna/projects/research-ai-goes-rural/), [**Robot Arm**](/educjenna/projects/research-robot-arm/), and [**CSforGood**](/educjenna/projects/research-csforgood/), collaborating with K–12 educators to design and study learning experiences in computing and AI. Her current first-author [**AI-Supported Lesson Planning**](/educjenna/projects/research-ai-lesson-planning/) research investigates how pre-service teachers regulate their use of AI-generated feedback during lesson planning.
