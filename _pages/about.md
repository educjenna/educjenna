<p>
  Juhyun Park is a third-year Ph.D. student in Instructional Systems Technology at Indiana University Bloomington and a former public elementary school teacher in Seoul, South Korea. Her research examines how learners think and learn with emerging technologies, with two primary areas of focus: <strong>embodied and tangible learning</strong> and <strong>GenAI-supported learning</strong>. As a graduate researcher, she contributes to NSF-funded and community-engaged projects including
  <a href="/educjenna/projects/research-silicon/"><strong>SILICON</strong></a>,
  <a href="/educjenna/projects/research-ai-goes-rural/"><strong>AI Goes Rural</strong></a>,
  <a href="/educjenna/projects/research-robot-arm/"><strong>Robot Arm</strong></a>, and
  <a href="/educjenna/projects/research-csforgood/"><strong>CSforGood</strong></a>,
  collaborating with K–12 educators to design and study learning experiences in computing and AI. Her current first-author
  <a href="/educjenna/projects/research-ai-lesson-planning/"><strong>AI-Supported Lesson Planning</strong></a>
  research investigates how pre-service teachers regulate their use of AI-generated feedback during lesson planning. 
</p>
