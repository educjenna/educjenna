Juhyun Park is a third-year Ph.D. student in **Instructional Systems Technology at Indiana University Bloomington** and a former public elementary school teacher in Seoul, South Korea. Her research asks how technology can support learners' thinking without doing that thinking for them, with a focus on young learners and novices, including pre-service teachers.

Her work spans a continuum of scaffolds: **embodied learning** that makes abstract computing and AI ideas tangible, and **self-regulated learning** that helps learners manage their own thinking with generative AI. As a graduate researcher on NSF-funded projects including **SILICON** and **AI Goes Rural**, she co-designs and studies learning experiences with K–12 teachers, and her first-author design-based study supports pre-service teachers in evaluating AI feedback during lesson planning. She teaches **EDUC-W200: Teaching with Technology** and has served as a teaching assistant for **EDUC-R521: Instructional Design and Development**.

Across these projects, one question guides her work:

> **When does technology scaffold learning?**

**Research interests:** Embodied Cognition · Self-regulated Learning · CS&AI Education · Teacher Education
