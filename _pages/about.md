I am a Ph.D. student in **Instructional Systems Technology at Indiana University Bloomington**, with a minor in **Qualitative and Quantitative Research Methodology**. My work spans artificial intelligence in education, teacher education, computational thinking, and the design of technology-supported learning experiences.

Before beginning my doctoral studies, I taught in public elementary schools in Seoul, South Korea. Across my research and teaching, I have worked with learners and educators from elementary school through university settings and in teacher professional development.

This site serves as both my academic portfolio and doctoral dossier. It documents my development across **research, teaching, and service**.

### Current appointments

- **Graduate Research Assistant**, NSF SILICON Project, Indiana University Bloomington (Fall 2025–Present)
- **Associate Instructor (Instructor of Record)**, School of Education, Indiana University Bloomington (Fall 2025–Present)

### Research interests

Artificial intelligence in education · Teacher education · Learning design · Computational thinking · AI literacy · Embodied learning
